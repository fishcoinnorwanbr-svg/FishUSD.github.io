# FishUSD.github.io

PAPER MONEY AND COINS IN THE HANDS OF CONSUMED AND BUSINESSES, AS OPPOSED TO CASH (VAULT CASH) HELD BY FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS, OR CURRENCY RESERVES OF FEDERAL RESERVE BANKS AND THE U.S TRASURY. CIRCULATIONF CURRENCY IS ONLY A SMALL FRACTION OF THE CASH AVALIABLE FOR SPENDING AND IT UNDERSTATES THE MONEU SUPPLY, WHICH IS LARGELY DEMAND DEPOSITS OR CHECKING ACCOUNTS IN BANKS ANS SAVINGS INSTITUIONS.
CURRENCY HELD BY THE PUBLIC IS ONE OF THE KEY FACTORS AFFECTING BANK RESERVES. IT IS ONE OF THE STATISTICAL MEASURES WATCHED BY THE FEDERAL OPEN MARKET COMMITEE TRANDING DESK AT THE FEDERAL RESERVE BANK OF NEW YORK WHEN THE FED DECIDES TO ADD OR DRAIN RESERVES FROM THE BANKING SYSTEM TO STAY WITHIN MONETARY POLICY TARGETS.
